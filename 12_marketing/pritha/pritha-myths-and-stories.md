# Pritha Myths And Stories

Status: draft

## Purpose

Collect future marketing language, myths, origin stories and product narrative
fragments for Pritha.

## Guardrail

This file is a creative surface. It is allowed to be warm, mythic and memorable,
but it is not evidence of what Pritha can do. Any capability claim must be
backed by standards, decisions, workflows, tests or reports before it appears in
serious product documentation.

## Seed Lines

- Pritha is not a single agent. She is the memory-aware workshop where agents
  are born with contracts, boundaries and a way to grow.
- Pritha remembers patterns, not raw noise.
- Pritha does not clone her children. She studies what worked, keeps the
  scaffolding honest and lets each descendant become itself.
