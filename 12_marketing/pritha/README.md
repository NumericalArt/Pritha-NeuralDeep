# Pritha Marketing

This section stores product narrative material about Pritha: positioning,
taglines, myths, stories, launch text, playful identity fragments and public
copy drafts.

Marketing artifacts are not standards. They may be aspirational, vivid or
mythic, but they must not claim capabilities that are not supported by curated
standards, decisions, workflows or reports.

Use this section for:

- "why Pritha matters" narratives;
- short product descriptions;
- myths and stories;
- demos and launch copy;
- brand voice experiments;
- public-facing explanations.

Do not use this section for:

- operational policy;
- security decisions;
- user private memory;
- unverified capability claims;
- raw incoming source material.
